\textbf{Model routing and cascades.}
FrugalGPT~\cite{chen2023frugalgpt} cascades models by a learned scorer; Hybrid LLM~\cite{ding2024hybrid} and RouteLLM~\cite{ong2024routellm} train routers on quality labels; AutoMix~\cite{aggarwal2023automix} lets a small model verify itself.
RouterBench~\cite{hu2024routerbench} provides the recorded outputs we route over.
We add general, untrained deciders to this comparison and find that none replaces a router trained on the target model's record.

\textbf{Semantic caching.}
GPTCache~\cite{bang2023gptcache} reuses answers above a similarity threshold.
vCache~\cite{schroeder2025vcache} learns per-prompt thresholds with an error bound and introduced the two traffic benchmarks we use.
We evaluate a judge that reads both queries in place of a similarity score, and we show that the benchmarks' class-based labels are unreliable for pairwise decisions.

\textbf{Small models for agent decisions.}
TinyAgent~\cite{erdogan2024tinyagent} and related work move tool selection to a small model with a larger model for execution.
Our in-loop arms test this split with a frontier generator on \taubench~\cite{yao2024taubench,barres2025tau2} and find that it does not beat a single call.

\textbf{Decision models.}
\jev~\cite{typesafe2026jev} and \kev~\cite{palmer2026kev} are recent; to our knowledge this is the first independent measurement of either across decision points with dollar, latency and energy costs.
